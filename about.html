About Bullz Eye Billiards

Crafting excellence since 2009 — where passion meets precision.

Our Heritage

Born from a Love of the Game

Founded in 2009 in Rajasthan, Bullz Eye Billiards began with a simple mission — to bring world-class billiards equipment to every corner of India. What started as a small workshop with two craftsmen has grown into one of India's most trusted names in gaming equipment manufacturing.

Over 15 years, we have supplied over 5,000 tables to prestigious clubs, five-star hotels, international resorts, gaming zones, and luxury homes across 200+ cities. Our commitment to quality has never wavered — every table leaves our facility only when it meets the highest standards.
